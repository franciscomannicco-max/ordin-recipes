Fried soya chunks

Description

You dont need much to follow this recipe. It is the cheapest recipe you will find on the internet today.

Ingredients

    A cup of soya chunks A tomato An onion Half a tablespoon of portuguese chicken spice Half a tablespoon of blackpaper Cooking oil Salt

Steps

    Soak your soya chunks in waater for about 3 minutes After you have soaked your chunks, put your chunks in a pan and half a cup of water. Boil the chunks until there is no water left Once the water is done, add some cooking oil to your chunks.Chunks may drain the oil so add 2 or three more times. Fry until the chunks turn golden brown Add the rest of the ingredients with your your desired amount of salt. Fry until there is some gravy seen.
